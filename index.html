Settings

Keep away from houses

Hidden, quiet & flat

From zoom 11 in the offline regions. “Hidden”: can people on roads, paths or in houses see or hear you? Forest, scrub and hedges hide you; open fields don't. Counts more than the house distance. “Quiet night”: road and railway noise, full penalty from 55 dB. “Avoid slopes”: from a 30 m elevation model, so small flat patches on a slope may not show.

Map data

Regions with offline data load instantly at any zoom.
Outside the offline regions, data is downloaded from the Overpass API (slow, shared service) automatically from zoom 13 and stays cached in the browser. Cells above the built-up threshold are counted instead of downloaded; the threshold applies to areas loaded from now on.

Offline use

Checking…
Stores buildings, water, terrain, protected areas, the hidden/noise maps and slopes for the visible area. The app itself and every area you've viewed also stay available offline. The background map only works offline where you've already looked at it (bulk downloads aren't allowed by OpenStreetMap), so for navigation without signal also export your spots as GPX to an offline map app.
iPhone: in Safari tap Share → “Add to Home Screen”. Installed, WildNav opens like an app and iOS keeps its stored data.
